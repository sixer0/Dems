## Focus answers

1. **Evidence path.**
   - The relay record path is unchanged in behaviour. `report()` keeps `RELAY_REPORT_NOT_WRITTEN` on EPIPE through `gone` (`relay.c` `out_bytes`/`report`). The record branch and `push_write` are unchanged, and preview mode is excluded from the re-open (`if (probed || !push.open || g_start.mode != RELAY_MODE_RECORD) break;`).
   - The record start message is unchanged (`relay_core.c`: the record seen-mask is still 15 and its source is still required).
   - Worker ingest and sealing are untouched, apart from T15 (`session-machine.ts:1244-1282`, `:1638`), which changes only `clipLatched` after SEALED (see #3).
   - The preview yields **before** any camera work of a recording. In `openSource` the yield is step 1 (`stream`, `capture-composition.ts:709` -> `onvif-relay.ts:663`): before the destination rule, the reveal, the locate and the relay. In the pre-check it comes before `probe.camera` (`capture-composition.ts:1104`).
   - The yield waits for the stop and reap, including an open in flight (`preview-manager.ts:334-347`). The generation counter drops late answers (`:419-421`).
   - It cannot hold the camera while the session opens, apart from the unbounded tail in #2. After the push stands it deliberately runs beside the recording (see #5).
   - The race between the 1 s watch and session CREATE / pre-check is covered for the card: `registered` (`capture-composition.ts:723`) awaits `stopAndWait` before the adapter's `storedRow` (`usb-audio-production.ts:591-594`). Enumerate, pre-check and open all pass through `storedRow` before writing the ALSA config. A remaining gap is #10a.
2. **Lifecycle.**

The encoder runs in its own process group (`relay-start.ts` `processGroup: true`). The relay ends cleanly on EPIPE or end of input. On Core close, `onClose` -> `service.closeAll()` + `provision.close()` (`modules/camera-preview/index.ts:50-53`; `capture-composition.ts:808-816`). The idle meters close the same way (`idle-meter.ts:455-464`).

   - Viewer caps hold. The **pipeline cap fails on resume** (#1). The reap bound is not enforced (#2).

## Key Challenges

- **Cap at resume.** "Recording has priority", and resuming on push-established is the one point where the cap is unprotected (#1). That resume is also the moment host CPU matters most for the evidence path.
- **Contract mismatch.** `CameraPreviewProcess.stop()` "resolves when the process has ended" is implemented faithfully by relay-start, but the opener treats it as "the order was given" (#2). One of the two needs to change, and the test fake has to follow whichever stays.
- **Wrapping vs replacing.** Should the pre-check yield replace the capture-notices probe source? Wrapping keeps the `NOT_ASKED` / 503 semantics that other rows pinned (#4).
- **Allowlist granularity.** A-9 was built as a file-level list. The three new entries widen it to whole modules where a symbol list would carry the stated intent ("no adapter of it") (#7).
- **Leg loss during recording.** The preview does not yield when the recording's camera leg is lost (#5). That needs either a code change or an explicit accepted-risk record.

## Positive Findings

- The door pipeline keeps "one spawn": both children start through the same start, under the same rules (absolute path, closed environment, core-file limit 0, kill at exit). No video byte passes the Core (`joint?.destroy()` after the second spawn).
- The credential reaches only the relay's start message. The encoder argv is literals plus one word from a closed two-codec table (`relay-start.ts` `previewEncoderArguments`), and no environment.
- The relay changes come with five new own-controls in `relay-check.sh`: a third writer caller, a third video place, reader-gone = stop, sets validated first, and the preview source required.
- The preview manager is careful about async state: generation counters, stops serialised through `before`, an in-flight open aborted and awaited, viewer back-pressure (`blocked` / `drained`), and a parser that cuts by length and resyncs.
- The third credential holder is added the closed-list way (`credential-reader.ts:86-100`), with a matching audit reason set (`audit-details.ts:198`).
- Token handling: compared in constant time, bound to operator and sign-in session, row committed before the token exists, never logged (`camera-preview/service.ts:183-187`, `:269-276`).
- The idle meters fail safe: `sessionActive` that throws counts as active (`idle-meter.ts:210-217`), and a start in flight is awaited by `stopAndWait` (`:440-447`).
- C1 (`capture-notices/probe.ts` `servedDevices`) closes D47 with a test that has a control. C4 (`registrar.ts` `READ_TAKEN_MICROPHONE_IDS`) closes D50.
